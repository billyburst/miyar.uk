About MIYAR LTD

Dedicated to bringing you the finest quality clothing since 2024

Our Story

Founded in June 2024, MIYAR LTD has quickly established itself as a trusted retailer of high-quality clothing in Tilbury, Essex. We are passionate about fashion and committed to providing our customers with exceptional products and outstanding service. Our carefully curated collections feature the latest trends and timeless classics, ensuring there's something for everyone.

Located at 15 Shelley Place in Tilbury, our physical store offers a personalised shopping experience where our knowledgeable team is always ready to help you find the perfect outfit. We pride ourselves on our attention to detail, quality craftsmanship, and customer satisfaction.
